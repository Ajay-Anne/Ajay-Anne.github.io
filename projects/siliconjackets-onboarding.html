SiliconJackets Onboarding Project

3 Stage Voltage Controlled Ring Oscillator and 6 Stage Buffer operating at 500 MHz.

My onboarding project involved designing a current starved voltage controlled ring oscillator operating at 500 MHz. As part of the project, I performed calculations to determine transistor sizing and input voltage, identifying load capacitances and current. This topology involves 3 inverters that are current starved with a current mirror. The input voltage controls the mirror's bias and thus changes the operating frequency. I also learned how to use Cadence Virtuoso to design schematics, simulate those schematics, and then create layouts. This project used the SKY130 PDK and rail voltages of 1.8 V for VDD and 0 V for GND. DRC and LVS checks passed for all layouts.

Oscillator

Above is the schematic and layout of the oscillator. During the process, I had to adjust sizings significantly from my calculations to adjust the waveforms and ensure the target frequency was met.

Buffer

The above buffer boosts the waveforms of the oscillator, ensuring they reach both rail voltages and fit the desired square wave shape. This buffer is 6 stages, using progessively larger inverters. To combat issues with my duty cycle, I did not scale up the buffer inverter sizings consistently. During the layout, I learned how to handle large transistors using multiple fingers.

Testbench and Waveform

This testbench was used to run simulations on my oscillator. In this testbench, the oscillator and buffer drive a 1 pF capacitor. The oscillation is slightly asymmetric with inconsistent rise and fall times which is undesirable.
