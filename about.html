About me

I'm a first-year Electrical Engineering student at the Georgia Institute of Technology originally from Greater Philadelphia. I'm interested in semiconductor devices and analog design. At Georgia Tech, I'm a member of SiliconJackets' Analog and Mixed-Signal Design Team and GT Solar Racing's Powertrain Subteam.

This semester, I've been taking coursework in signal processing and digital design. Next semester, I plan on expanding my knowledge with courses in circuit analysis, hardware programming, statistics, and a digital design lab.

Beyond the classroom

Georgia Tech Solar Racing

I'm on the Powertrain subteam, where I assist the team with integrating the motors into the car. Current and past projects include working on internal team libraries for STM32G4 firmware development and physical harnessing, including crimping, board fabrication/testing, and more.

SiliconJackets

I'm a member of the Analog Mixed-Signal Subteam. Currently, I'm working on designing the sizing, schematic, and layout of a current starved inverter operational amplifier. Previously, I implemented a 3-stage voltage controlled ring oscillator and 6-stage buffer operating at 500 MHz.

Violin

I started playing the violin when I was just 4 years old. Since then, I've had the opportunity to perform and hold leadership roles in many orchestras. I've served as concertmaster of my school orchestras, the WCASD Senior Strings, and Chester County Youth Orchestra. I've also had the pleasure of playing in PMEA All District and All Region Orchestras. I am currently a member of the Georgia Tech Symphony Orchestra.

Scouting

I earned the rank of Eagle Scout in 2024. My Eagle Project involved planting over 200 trees at Mt. Bradford Preserve in West Chester, PA. I also served my troop during various periods as Webmaster, Senior Patrol Leader, and Jr. Assistant Scoutmaster. In 2026, I earned scouting's Distinguished Conservation Service Award, having completed an additional project creating a pollinator meadow.
